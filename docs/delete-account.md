# Questline · Delete Your Account

**Last updated:** 2026-05-14

This page explains how to delete your Questline account and what happens to your data when you do.

## Two ways to delete

### Inside the app (recommended)

If you still have Questline installed:

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

The deletion runs immediately. You'll be signed out and your account is gone.

### By email (if you no longer have the app)

If you've already uninstalled Questline or can't sign in, email us at:

**questline.customerservice@gmail.com**

Include the email address you used to register so we can locate your account. We'll delete your account within 7 days of your request and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted

Within 24 hours of your request, every record associated with your account is removed from our database, including:

- Your profile (email, character name, title, factions, campaigns, background, life summary)
- All quests you created (titles, descriptions, deadlines, objectives)
- Your activity history (XP balance, level history, streak counts, completion timestamps)
- Modifiers (buffs and debuffs you've earned)
- Achievement progress
- Push notification tokens
- AI usage logs older than 90 days are already deleted on a rolling schedule; remaining logs are deleted with your account

## What may be retained

A small amount of data may be retained after account deletion when required by law:

- **Subscription / billing records** are retained for tax and accounting purposes for the period required by law (typically 7 years). These records contain your account ID and subscription state, but you can request deletion of personally identifying information from them by emailing us.
- **System logs** (request timestamps, error traces) may persist in our infrastructure for up to 30 days before automatic rotation, but are not linked to your identity in a way that allows re-identification.

We never share, sell, or use your data for advertising — see our [Privacy Policy](/Questline/privacy/) for details on what we collect and how we use it.

## Subscription cancellation

Deleting your account does **not** automatically cancel an active Hero subscription. To cancel:

- **Google Play:** Open the Play Store app → tap your profile → **Payments and subscriptions** → **Subscriptions** → select Questline → **Cancel subscription**.
- **Apple App Store:** Open Settings → tap your name → **Subscriptions** → select Questline → **Cancel subscription**.

We recommend canceling your subscription **before** deleting your account so you aren't charged for a tier you can no longer access.

## Questions

Email **questline.customerservice@gmail.com** for any questions about account deletion or your data.
